Fuji Omuro Sengen Shrine

冨士御室浅間神社

Shinto shrine · Yamanashi · ✓ Checked on the official website

About

Fuji Omuro Sengen Shrine is a Shinto shrine in Yamanashi Prefecture, Japan. It is traditionally visited for fertility & safe birth.
