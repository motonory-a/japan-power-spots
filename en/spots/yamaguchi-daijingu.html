Yamaguchi Daijingū

山口大神宮

Shinto shrine · Yamaguchi · ✓ Checked on the official website

About

Yamaguchi Daijingū is a Shinto shrine in Yamaguchi Prefecture, Japan. It is traditionally visited for good fortune, business success, money luck.
