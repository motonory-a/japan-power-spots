Ōmiya Hachiman Shrine

大宮八幡宮

Shinto shrine · Hyogo

About

Ōmiya Hachiman Shrine is a Shinto shrine in Miki, Hyōgo Prefecture, Japan. It is a Hachiman Shrine that was founded in 1111 and was rebuilt in 1585 after having burned down.
