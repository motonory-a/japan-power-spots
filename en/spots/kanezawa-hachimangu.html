Kanezawa Hachimangū

金澤八幡宮

Shinto shrine · Akita · ✓ Checked on the official website

About

Kanezawa Hachimangū is a Shinto shrine in Akita Prefecture, Japan. It is traditionally visited for winning luck, protection from bad luck.
