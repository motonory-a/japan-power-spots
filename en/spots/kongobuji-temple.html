Kongobuji Temple

金剛峯寺

Buddhist temple · Wakayama · ✓ Checked on the official website

About

Kongōbu-ji (金剛峯寺) is the ecclesiastic head temple of Kōyasan Shingon Buddhism, located on Mount Kōya , Wakayama Prefecture, Japan. Its name means Vajra Peak Temple. It is part of the "Sacred Sites and Pilgrimage Routes in the Kii Mountain Range" UNESCO World Heritage Site.

After your visit: one local bite

Goma-dofu (sesame tofu) 胡麻豆腐

Temple-cuisine sesame "tofu" made with Yoshino kudzu and Koyasan water, by a shop founded in the early Meiji era. Mostly sold to take away; may sell out.

Hamadaya 濱田屋
