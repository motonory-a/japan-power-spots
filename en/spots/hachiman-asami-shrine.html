Hachiman Asami Shrine

八幡朝見神社

Shinto shrine · Oita · ✓ Checked on the official website

About

Hachiman Asami Shrine is a Shinto shrine in Oita Prefecture, Japan. It is traditionally visited for winning luck, fertility & safe birth, protection from bad luck.
