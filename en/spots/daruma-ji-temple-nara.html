Daruma-ji Temple

達磨寺

Buddhist temple · Nara

About

Daruma-ji is a Zen Buddhist temple in the city of Ōji in the Kitakatsuragi District, Nara Prefecture, Japan and is one of the 28 historical Sites of Prince Shōtoku.
