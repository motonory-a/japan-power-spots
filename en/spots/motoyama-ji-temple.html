Motoyama-ji Temple

本山寺

Buddhist temple · Kagawa · ✓ Checked on the official website

About

The Shippōzan Motoyama-ji is a Shingon Buddhist temple of the Kōyasan sect in Mitoyo, Kagawa Prefecture, Japan. It was established by Emperor Heizei's order in 807. Hayagriva is the principal image. The temple has undergone several reconstruction efforts since its founding, including the rebuilding of its Main Hall in 1300.
