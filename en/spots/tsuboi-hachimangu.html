Tsuboi Hachimangū

壺井八幡宮

Shinto shrine · Osaka

About

Tsuboi Hachimangū is a Shinto shrine located in Habikino, Osaka Prefecture, Japan. It is a Hachiman shrine, dedicated to the kami Hachiman. It was established in 1063 by Minamoto no Yoriyoshi. Its main festival is held annually on May 15.
