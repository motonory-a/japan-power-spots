Zenkō-ji Temple

善光寺

Buddhist temple · Nagano · ✓ Checked on the official website

About

Zenkō-ji is a Buddhist temple located in the city of Nagano, Japan. The temple was built in the 7th century. It is one of the few remaining pilgrimage sites in Japan. The modern city of Nagano began as a town built around the temple.

After your visit: one local bite

Miso-grilled rice ball のり巻 焼むすび(味噌)

A rice ball grilled with Shinshu miso and wrapped in nori, made in front of you by a miso maker founded in 1902. Try the miso soft-serve too.

Suyakame Zenkoji store 門前みそ すや亀 善光寺店 · since 1902
At the Nakamise entrance, inside the Niomon gate

Checked in 2026/11 that the shop still sells it (source 1, source 2). Opening days change — please check before you go. Photo (a typical miso-grilled rice ball, not necessarily this shop's): Janine from Mililani, Hawaii, United States, CC BY 2.0, Wikimedia Commons.
