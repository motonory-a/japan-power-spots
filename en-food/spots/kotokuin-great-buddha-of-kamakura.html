Kotokuin (Great Buddha of Kamakura)

高徳院

Buddhist temple · Kanagawa · ✓ Checked on the official website

About

Kōtoku-in (高徳院) is a Buddhist temple of the Jōdo-shū sect in the city of Kamakura in Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan. Its mountain name is Daii-zan (大異山), and its common temple name is Shōjōsen-ji (清浄泉寺).

After your visit: one local bite

Gongoro chikara mochi 権五郎力餅

Rice cake pounded every morning, covered with smooth red-bean paste. No additives — eat it the same day. Closed Wednesdays and the 3rd Tuesday.

Chikaramochiya 力餅家
By Goryo Shrine, 6–7 min from Hase Station
