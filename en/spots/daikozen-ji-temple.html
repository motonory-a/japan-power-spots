Daikōzen-ji Temple

大興善寺

Buddhist temple · Saga

About

Daikōzen-ji (大興善寺) is a Tendai temple in Kiyama, Saga Prefecture, Japan. Its honorary sangō prefix is Komatsuzan (小松山).
